# FamilyOS — Pilot 1 Productization Plan

## Status

- Version: 1.0
- State: Proposed productization sequence

## Product Direction

Pilot 1 should convert validated Pilot 0 learning into repeatable daily utility.

Productization should prioritize:

1. usefulness;
2. reliability;
3. understandable privacy;
4. low-friction onboarding;
5. safe automation;
6. measurable learning.

## Stage 1 — Daily Utility

Focus on a small number of high-frequency family jobs:

- shared agenda and coordination;
- reminders / due-date awareness;
- document and administrative organization;
- family brief / summary;
- conflict detection and confirmation.

## Stage 2 — Repeatability

Make the useful path repeatable:

- deterministic onboarding;
- stable family context;
- clear data ownership;
- recoverable persistence;
- predictable operator support;
- measurable failure rates.

## Stage 3 — Trust

Expose trust as product behavior:

- who can see what;
- why access exists;
- what data was used;
- what external service was used;
- how to delete/export;
- how to withdraw permission.

## Stage 4 — Intelligence

Only after the daily-use loop is reliable:

- family intelligence;
- proactive suggestions;
- document expiration awareness;
- weekly brief;
- decision support.

Human confirmation remains required wherever the risk model demands it.

## Stage 5 — Ecosystem

Only after stable product usage:

- connectors;
- skills;
- plugin marketplace;
- broader provider support;
- partner integrations.

## Evidence Metrics

Pilot 1 should measure at minimum:

- active families;
- recurring weekly usage;
- useful-action completion;
- confirmation/rejection rate;
- failed extraction or automation rate;
- conflict-detection usefulness;
- onboarding completion;
- support burden;
- privacy/authorization incidents;
- provider cost;
- willingness-to-pay signals.

## Productization Exit

Productization is ready to move beyond Pilot 1 when:

- daily utility is repeatable;
- safety boundaries survive normal use;
- onboarding is reproducible;
- operational cost is understood;
- retention signals justify further investment;
- the next architecture investment is supported by evidence.
